Circular orbit conditions

Section titled “Circular orbit conditions”

  • r=constantr = \text{constant}r=constant

  • r¨=0\ddot r = 0r¨=0

  • No radiale drift
  • centripetal balance exact

Important result

Section titled “Important result”

A stable circular orbit only exists if r>3Mr > 3Mr>3M!

For:

  • r=3Mr = 3Mr=3M → photon sphere (instable light orbit)
  • r<3Mr < 3Mr<3M → no stable circular orbit possible
