### Coulomb force
<div class="header_line"></div>

This page contains various various simulations that both calculate and show the motion of a charged particle
in various electric fields, such as a charged ring and charged plates. The trajectory of such a particle is
then determined by the Coulomb force:

$$
\overrightarrow{F}_C = q \overrightarrow{E}
$$
