### 🌕 Mercury
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/mercury.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=mercury.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Smallest planet in the solar system.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Around -180°C at night and 430°C during the day.<br/>
📌 **Year**: Orbits the Sun in just 88 Earth days.<br/>
📌 **Surface**: Covered in craters, resembling our Moon.<br/>
📌 **Gravity**: About 38% of Earth's gravity.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Despite being closest to the Sun, Venus is hotter because of its extreme greenhouse effect.<br/>

### ♀️ Venus
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/venus.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=venus.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Almost identical to Earth.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Approximately 465°C on average.<br/>
📌 **Atmosphere**: Extremely dense, mostly carbon dioxide.<br/>
📌 **Pressure**: About 92 times Earth's atmospheric pressure at the surface.<br/>
📌 **Rotation**: Rotates backwards compared with most planets.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: A day on Venus (one rotation) is longer than its year (one orbit around the Sun).<br/>

### 🌍 Earth
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/earth.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=earth.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: The fifth-largest planet.<br/>
📌 **Water**: Approximately 71% of its surface is covered by water.<br/>
📌 **Atmosphere**: Mainly nitrogen and oxygen.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Average surface temperature is approximately 15°C.<br/>
📌 **Moon**: Has one natural satellite.<br/>
📌 **Magnetic field**: Protects us from much of the solar wind and charged particles.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Earth's liquid water, protective atmosphere and suitable temperatures make it uniquely habitable.<br/>

### ♂️ Mars
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/mars.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=mars.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Approximately half Earth's diameter.<br/>
📌 **Colour**: Red because of iron oxide (rust) in its surface dust.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Average around -65°C.<br/>
📌 **Moons**: Two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.<br/>
📌 **Volcano**: Home to Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the solar system.<br/>
📌 **Day**: Almost identical to Earth's, lasting approximately 24 hours and 37 minutes.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Mars has evidence of ancient rivers and lakes, suggesting that liquid water once flowed across its surface.<br/>

### 🌪️ Jupiter
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/jupiter.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=jupiter.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: The largest planet in our solar system.<br/>
📌 **Mass**: More than twice the combined mass of all the other planets.<br/>
📌 **Composition**: Primarily hydrogen and helium.<br/>
📌 **Storms**: Home to the Great Red Spot, a gigantic storm that has lasted for centuries.<br/>
📌 **Moons**: Has a large family of moons, including Europa, Ganymede and Io.<br/>
📌 **Magnetic field**: Has an extremely powerful magnetic field.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Ganymede, one of Jupiter's moons, is actually larger than the planet Mercury.<br/>

### 🪐 Saturn
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/saturn.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=saturn.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Second-largest planet.<br/>
📌 **Rings**: Has spectacular rings made primarily of ice particles, rocks and dust.<br/>
📌 **Density**: Less dense than water, meaning a hypothetical giant ocean could theoretically float Saturn.<br/>
📌 **Atmosphere**: Mostly hydrogen and helium.<br/>
📌 **Moons**: Has numerous moons, including Titan and Enceladus.<br/>
📌 **Titan**: Has lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Enceladus shoots enormous plumes of water vapour and ice into space, suggesting a potentially habitable ocean beneath its icy crust.<br/>

### ❄️ Uranus
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/uranus.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=uranus.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Third-largest planet.<br/>
📌 **Colour**: Pale blue-green due to methane in its atmosphere.<br/>
📌 **Rotation**: Rotates almost on its side, with an axial tilt of approximately 98 degrees.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Its upper atmosphere reaches approximately -224°C, making it the coldest planetary atmosphere measured in our solar system.<br/>
📌 **Rings**: Has 13 known rings.<br/>
📌 **Moons**: Has 28 known moons.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Because of its extreme tilt, parts of Uranus experience approximately 42 years of continuous sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness.<br/>

### 🔱 Neptune
<div class="header_line"></div>

<div class="badgeRow">
    <a href="https://github.com/zhendrikse/helion/blob/main/examples/astrophysics/scenes/planets/neptune.js">
        <img alt="Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/github-repo-green?logo=github&label=neptune.js"/>
    </a>
</div>

📌 **Size**: Fourth-largest planet.<br/>
📌 **Distance**: The farthest officially recognized planet from the Sun.<br/>
📌 **Colour**: Deep blue, partly due to methane in its atmosphere and other atmospheric factors.<br/>
📌 **Temperature**: Approximately -200°C in its upper atmosphere.<br/>
📌 **Winds**: Has some of the fastest planetary winds, reaching approximately 2,000 km/h.<br/>
📌 **Year**: Takes approximately 165 Earth years to orbit the Sun.<br/>
📌 **Moon**: Its largest moon, Triton, orbits backwards relative to Neptune's rotation.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: Neptune was discovered through mathematical predictions before it was directly observed through a telescope.<br/>
