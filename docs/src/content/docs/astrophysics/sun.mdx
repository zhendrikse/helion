📌 **Type**: G2V star, commonly called a yellow dwarf.<br/>
📌 **Age**: Approximately 4.6 billion years.<br/>
📌 **Diameter**: Approximately 1.39 million kilometres.<br/>
📌 **Mass**: Approximately 333,000 times the mass of Earth.<br/>
📌 **Distance** from Earth: Approximately 150 million kilometres.<br/>
📌 **Temperature** at visible surface: Approximately 5,500°C.<br/>
📌 **Core** temperature: Approximately 15 million°C.<br/>
📌 **Composition**: Primarily hydrogen (about 73% by mass) and helium (about 25%), with small amounts of heavier elements.<br/>
📌 **Gravity**: Approximately 28 times Earth's surface gravity.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: More than 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun by volume!<br/>

The Sun produces energy through a process called nuclear fusion.

📌 Its enormous gravitational pressure compresses hydrogen in its core.<br/>
📌 Hydrogen nuclei combine to form helium.<br/>
📌 A small amount of mass is converted into enormous amounts of energy.<br/>
📌 This energy eventually escapes into space as light and other electromagnetic radiation.<br/>
📌 That energy travels approximately 150 million kilometres to reach Earth in just over 8 minutes.<br/>
👉 **Remarkable fact**: The Sun converts approximately 600 million tonnes of hydrogen into helium every second. Around 4 million tonnes of mass are converted into energy every second.<br/>
