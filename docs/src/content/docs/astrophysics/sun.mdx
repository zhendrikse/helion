- **Type**: G2V star, commonly called a yellow dwarf.
- **Age**: Approximately 4.6 billion years.
- **Diameter**: Approximately 1.39 million kilometres.
- **Mass**: Approximately 333,000 times the mass of Earth.
- **Distance** from Earth: Approximately 150 million kilometres.
- **Temperature** at visible surface: Approximately 5,500°C.
- **Core** temperature: Approximately 15 million°C.
- **Composition**: Primarily hydrogen (about 73% by mass) and helium (about 25%), with small amounts of heavier elements.
- **Gravity**: Approximately 28 times Earth's surface gravity.
- **Remarkable fact**: More than 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun by volume!

The Sun produces energy through a process called nuclear fusion.

- Its enormous gravitational pressure compresses hydrogen in its core.
- Hydrogen nuclei combine to form helium.
- A small amount of mass is converted into enormous amounts of energy.
- This energy eventually escapes into space as light and other electromagnetic radiation.
- That energy travels approximately 150 million kilometres to reach Earth in just over 8 minutes.
- **Remarkable fact**: The Sun converts approximately 600 million tonnes of hydrogen into helium every second. Around 4 million tonnes of mass are converted into energy every second.
