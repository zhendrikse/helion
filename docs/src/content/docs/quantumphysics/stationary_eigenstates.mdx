### What are you looking at?
<div class="header_line"><br/></div>

This demo visualizes the stationary eigenstates of a quantum system.
Select different eigenstates to explore how their amplitude and phase form distinct
wave-like patterns, including the characteristic lobes and nodes of quantum wavefunctions.
