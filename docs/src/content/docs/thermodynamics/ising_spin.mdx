## Ising spin
<div class="header_line"><br/></div>

- The model walks through a temperature range between 0.1 and 4.9.
Per temperature the programs executes 50 relaxation sweeps after which it
determines the average of 100 measurements are being averaged.
- Spins evolve with the [Metropolis-algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis%E2%80%93Hastings_algorithm),
including periodic boundary conditions.
- The plot depicts the average absolute magnetization per spin as function of the temperature.
- State is reinitialized at every new temperature, not after every sweep!
