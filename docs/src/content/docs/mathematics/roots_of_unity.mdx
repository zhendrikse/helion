### About
<div class="header_line"></div>

The $n$-th roots of unity  are the complex numbers $z$ that satisfy the equation:

$$
z^n =1\quad \text{or equivalently}\quad z^n -1 = 0
$$

using Euler's formula, we can find all $n$ distinct roots. They are given by:

$$
z_k=e^{i\dfrac{2\pi k}{n}}=\cos\left(\dfrac{2\pi k}{n}\right) + i\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi k}{n}\right), \quad k=0, 1, 2,\cdots,n-1.
$$
