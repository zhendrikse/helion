From [Why Is a Sphere’s Surface Area 4πr²? Visual Proof, No Calculus](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/qnKEn3Obxbs):

Why is the surface area of a sphere exactly 4πr²?
Archimedes found the answer over 2,000 years ago, with no calculus.

In this 2-minute visual proof, you'll see:

🍊 Why the skin of a sphere covers exactly 4 circles of the same radius<br/>
📐 How Archimedes compared a sphere to a cylinder (a can) wrapped around it<br/>
🔍 Why a patch of the sphere and its "shadow" on the can's wall have the same area: where one stretches, the other shrinks<br/>
🧮 Why the can's wall is $2\pi r \cdot 2r = 4\pi r^2$<br/>

Key idea: sphere's skin = can's wall, so they have the same area.

- 0:00 The orange and the paper circles
- 0:20 Archimedes and the can
- 0:40 Patch and shadow: stretched sideways, squeezed toward the pole
- 1:20 The can's wall = 4πr²
- 1:50 Why this equals 4 circles
